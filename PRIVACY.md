# Privacy Policy for SlideGrabber

**Last updated: 14 July 2026**

SlideGrabber is a Chrome extension that captures screenshots of the browser tab
you are viewing and saves them to your local Downloads folder. This policy
explains what the extension does and does not do with your data.

## Summary

**SlideGrabber does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**
Everything the extension does happens locally on your own computer. There are no
servers, no analytics, no tracking, and no third parties involved.

## What data the extension handles

- **Screenshots of the active tab.** When you click **Start** (or **Capture
  preview**), the extension captures the visible area of the current tab as an
  image. These images are written directly to your local Downloads folder. They
  are never uploaded or sent anywhere. SlideGrabber does not read, inspect, or
  analyze the contents of the images.

- **Your settings.** The values you enter in the side panel (file name, target
  folder, format, crop values, delays, etc.) are stored locally on your device
  using Chrome's `storage` API so they are remembered between sessions. These
  settings never leave your computer and contain no personal information.

## What the extension does NOT do

- It does **not** collect or transmit any personal or sensitive information.
- It does **not** use analytics, telemetry, cookies, or tracking of any kind.
- It does **not** send data to the developer or to any third-party server.
- It does **not** sell or share any data with anyone.
- It does **not** monitor your browsing history or activity outside the moment
  you actively use it to capture screenshots.

## Permissions

SlideGrabber requests only the permissions required for its screenshot
functionality (capturing the visible tab, saving files to Downloads, sending a
key press to advance the page, storing your settings, and showing the side
panel). None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data. A
detailed justification for each permission is available in the project's store
documentation.

## Data storage and retention

- Screenshots are saved to your local Downloads folder and remain fully under
  your control. You can delete them at any time.
- Settings are stored locally by Chrome and are removed when you uninstall the
  extension.

The developer has no access to any of this data.

## Children's privacy

SlideGrabber does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including
children.
